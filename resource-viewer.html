Kharif 2081 Season Guide

Monsoon Maize Sowing & Nitrogen Soil Preparation Guide

Published by: Nepal Agriculture Research Council (NARC) · Bagmati Province Extension Office

📅 Shrawan 2081📄 12 pages🌐 English + नेपाली⬇ 2,847 downloads

1 Introduction & Seasonal Context

Kharif maize cultivation in Bagmati Province hill districts (1,000–2,000 masl) presents unique challenges and opportunities. The monsoon season (Ashad–Bhadra) provides adequate rainfall of 800–1,200mm, but poor soil nitrogen and drainage management remain the primary yield-limiting factors for smallholder farmers.

🌾 Key Target: 6–8 MT/ha yield

With proper nitrogen management and sowing timing, Kavre district farmers can achieve 6–8 MT/ha compared to the current average of 3.8 MT/ha.

3 Nitrogen Application Protocol

Nitrogen (N) is the single most yield-limiting nutrient in monsoon maize. The recommended total dose is 120–150 kg N/ha, split across three applications:

1

Basal dose (at sowing)

30 kg N/ha as DAP (65 kg DAP/ha). Mix thoroughly with soil before dibbling.

2

First top dressing (V4–V6 stage)

50 kg N/ha as Urea (109 kg Urea/ha). Apply 5 cm from base, cover with soil.

3

Second top dressing (V8–V10, pre-tasseling)

40–70 kg N/ha depending on crop colour. If leaves are dark green, reduce by 20%.

4 Row Spacing & Plant Population

Use 75 cm × 25 cm row-plant spacing for flat terrain (53,000 plants/ha). On slopes, use 60 cm × 25 cm across the slope. Target 2 seeds per hole, thin to 1 plant per station at V3 stage.

5 Drainage & Water Management

Construct field channels every 2–3 rows on slopes. Maize is highly sensitive to waterlogging — even 24 hours of standing water at knee height can reduce yield by 30–50%. Maintain 50 cm wide drainage furrows along contour lines.

⚠ Kavre District Warning — Monsoon 2081

DHM forecasts above-normal rainfall in Kavre through Bhadra 2081. Prepare drainage channels before sowing. Consider raised-bed planting in poorly-drained plots.
