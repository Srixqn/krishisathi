About This Disease

Early Blight (Alternaria solani) causes dark brown concentric ring lesions on older leaves. High humidity (78%) and warm temperatures (24°C) currently present in Kavre match outbreak criteria.

Immediate Remedies — Act Within 48 Hours

1

Apply Copper Oxychloride (Blitox 50 WP @ 3g/L)

Spray both leaf surfaces. Repeat every 7 days. Effective against Alternaria.

2

Remove & Destroy Infected Leaves

Hand-pick leaves with >30% lesion cover. Burn off-site. Do NOT compost.

3

Improve Air Circulation

Stake plants. Remove lower leaves below 30cm from soil. Avoid evening irrigation.

4

Potassium Silicate Foliar Spray (2g/L)

Strengthens cell walls. Reduces infection severity by 15–25%. Apply weekly.
